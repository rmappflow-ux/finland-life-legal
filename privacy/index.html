Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

In short: Finland Life has no account and no login, and no Finland Life server receives your data. What you do in the app stays on your device. When the app uses the internet, it asks public data services (weather, trains, news and so on) directly from your device. We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools, we do not track you, and we do not sell data.

1. Who we are

Finland Life is published by Rafael Melo. Privacy contact: rmappflow@gmail.com.

2. What stays only on your device

  • Your profile and choices: name or nickname you type, life mode, city, trip dates, interests, language and appearance.
  • Your progress: Finnish lessons, practice, streak and daily points, passport stamps, saved places, saved news, curiosities and events, travel list and trip plans, checklist and reminders.
  • Aurora sightings you log, including the note and any photo you attach (the photo is copied into the app's storage on your device).
  • Your saved commute (stations and times) for "Leave in 10 minutes".
  • The offline trip copy you download (weather and aurora forecasts for one place; at most 2 MB, deleted automatically after the trip).
  • Temporary copies of downloaded public data (forecasts, timetables, news, photos), kept in the device's cache.

Sync with iCloud is currently switched off: your data is not copied to iCloud by the app. The device's own iCloud or computer backup may include the app's files, under Apple's rules.

3. Location

  • The app asks for location only "while using the app". It never tracks you in the background.
  • It is used to show local weather, aurora conditions, the nearest train station, road camera and tide gauge, and to plan trips by public transport.
  • For the "Aurora overhead now" alert (if you switch it on), iOS may wake the app in the background now and then. The app then uses the last location iOS already has, without asking for a new one, and works out the nearest magnetometer station on the device. Your location is not sent anywhere in that check.
  • When a service needs a place (weather forecast, route planner), the app sends the coordinates of your location or of the place you chose. See section 6.
  • You can deny or withdraw the permission in iPhone Settings; the app keeps working with the city you choose.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

Only in speaking exercises, when you tap to speak. The app uses Apple's speech recognition to turn what you say into text and compare it with the phrase. When the iPhone supports it, recognition runs on the device; otherwise Apple processes the audio under its own privacy policy. The app never saves your voice.

5. Calendar, notifications and background refresh

  • Calendar: write-only, and only when you tap to add a trip or event; the app cannot read your calendar.
  • Notifications are local, scheduled on the device (word of the day, practice reminder, delayed trains, aurora alerts, commute and checklist reminders). There are no push notifications and no notification server. Each kind can be switched off in the app.
  • Background app refresh is used only for the "Aurora overhead now" check described in section 3.

7. Purchases (Sisu)

Sisu subscriptions and 7-day passes are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees or stores payment details; it only asks the App Store which purchases are active on your Apple Account. Cancellation and refunds follow Apple's rules.

8. External links

The app opens sites outside it: news articles, official services (for example Migri, DVV, Kela, Vero, suomi.fi), airline sites and Google Flights, maps, Instagram (@thefinlandlife) and these legal pages. Their own privacy policies and terms apply there.

9. Export and delete your data

In Settings › Your data you can export a file with everything the app keeps, and delete it all with "Delete local data" (see the Delete your data page). Uninstalling the app also removes it. There is nothing to delete on our side, because we hold no copy.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

11. International transfer

The services in section 6 are in Finland, the European Union, the United States and elsewhere, and may process connection data outside your country.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Brazil's LGPD give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your data, to object, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because Finland Life keeps your data only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (section 9). For any question, write to rmappflow@gmail.com; we reply within 15 days.

13. Changes to this policy

When the policy changes, the date at the top is updated; if the change is significant, we will let you know in the app.
